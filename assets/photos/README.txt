Put your photos in this folder, named exactly:

  hero.jpg        Home page, main image (portrait works best)
  meet-greet.jpg  Home page, "how it works" section (portrait)
  shivonne.jpg    About page (portrait, ideally Shivonne with a cat)
  01.jpg ... 09.jpg   The nine gallery tiles (square)

Names are case sensitive. Use .jpg, not .JPG or .jpeg.

Any slot with no photo simply shows the peach placeholder — nothing breaks,
so you can add them one at a time.

Resize before uploading. A photo straight off a phone is 3-5MB and will make
the site crawl. Aim for under 400KB and around 1200px on the long edge.
squoosh.app does this free in a browser.
