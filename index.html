Why a sitter, not a cattery

Cats don’t miss you as much as they miss their own house

Cats bond to territory. Take that away and you get a stressed cat in a strange room full of unfamiliar smells. Keeping them at home removes almost all of it.

No carrier, no car journey

For a lot of cats the trip is the worst part of the whole thing. This way it never happens.

The routine doesn’t change

Same food, same bowls, same sunny spot at two o’clock, same litter tray in the same corner.

Someone’s checking the house too

Post picked up, curtains and lights moved about, plants watered if you like. It looks lived in.

How it works

Three steps and you’re booked

  1. Say hello

    Message on WhatsApp or Instagram with your dates and a bit about your cat. Shivonne will confirm whether those dates are free.

  2. Free meet and greet

    She comes to you, meets your cat, learns the routine and picks up a key. No obligation and no charge.

  3. Go away, get updates

    Payment by bank transfer the day before the first visit, then photos and a short update after every single visit.
